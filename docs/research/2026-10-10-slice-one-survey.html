Slice one: which event?

Looked at YouTube copies of 1999–2003 broadcasts, with Liquipedia game counts as the baseline. Sample-based: we checked format details on a few videos per source, not every video.

Headline: the premise is half-wrong

OGN's early StarLeague (OSL) is already preserved by OGN. The official channel @TVOngamenet has per-season playlists for OSL 1999–2011. In 2026 it also started posting AI-upscaled 1080p versions of the 1999–2001 seasons.

MBC Game is the real gap. The channel shut down in 2012 and there's no official archive. Its successor, MBCplus, has no StarCraft playlists. What's left is scattered fan uploads at 240–288p and 15fps.

That shifts the project. For OSL the job becomes indexing and mirroring: map every official upload to the right game, then mirror it in case the channel disappears. Restoration matters most for MBC Game.

Options

recommended

A · 2002 KPGA Tour finals (MBC Game)

  • The project's thesis holds here: there's no official copy, and the fan copies are 240–288p at 15fps.
  • It's iconic: the 2002-04-14 1st Tour final is Hong Jin-ho vs Lim Yo-hwan (BoxeR). Four tours give 5–10 finals games.
  • It tests the hard parts early: messy sources, identifying which games are in multi-game uploads, and gaps.
  • Risk: some finals games may not be findable anywhere. That gap is a useful result too.

B · 2003 TG Sambo MSL (MBC Game)

  • There are more copies, with some 480p/30, so it's an easier first restoration.
  • The famous final (YellOw vs iloveoov, U-Boat) has several competing copies, which makes a good case for picking the best one.
  • The gap is smaller, so it proves less.

C · 2001 SKY OSL (OGN)

  • The easiest option. It would be a mirror-and-index slice that compares OGN's official upload, OGN's AI upscale, and our conservative restore.
  • The comparison is interesting in its own right: our archival-correct restore vs OGN's AI upscale.
  • It doesn't fill a real gap.

Still unverified

  • Game counts for the MBC Game events. Liquipedia hasn't been queried for them yet.
  • Whether OGN's playlists actually cover every game. 82 videos vs ~99 games for Freechal suggests some are missing; this needs a game-by-game match.
  • What source OGN's AI upscale was made from, and whether it invents detail. Check that before ranking it.
  • Pre-1999 shows and Proleague haven't been surveyed.
